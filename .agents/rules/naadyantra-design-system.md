# NAADYANTRA Design System & Aesthetic Token Directives

## Design Philosophy
Cinematic light industrial: refined typography, warm ambient lighting, precise technical graphics, clean layouts, and restrained orange emphasis.

## Palette
- **Industrial Ivory**: `#F7F5F0` (Default background)
- **Pure White**: `#FFFFFF` (Card surfaces, elevated components)
- **Warm Stone**: `#E9E5DE` (Dividers, borders, subtle lines)
- **Carbon**: `#252525` (High-emphasis text, high contrast icons)
- **Warm Graphite**: `#716D67` (Medium-emphasis text, units, secondary labels)
- **Burnt Orange**: `#F07842` (Primary brand accent, primary CTA)
- **Dark Burnt Orange**: `#A94E29` (Active/pressed states, warning borders)
- **Soft Sand**: `#D8C9BA` (Chip backgrounds, muted highlights)

## Absolute Constraints
- **NO Generic Blues, Cyans, or Teals**: Under no circumstances should cyan, teal, or generic default Material blue accents be introduced.
- **Data Clarity**: Diagnostic waveforms, acoustic frequency plots, and valve leakage figures must be crisp, legible, and accompanied by calibrated engineering units.
- **Touch & Accessibility**: Interactive targets must be at least 48x48 pt.
